# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

## Purpose
Stores important cross-repository architectural and technical decisions.

## What belongs here
Accepted/rejected decisions with context, rationale, alternatives, and consequences.

## Who should use this
Engineering contributors and AI agents when making or consuming non-trivial design decisions.

## What should NOT be stored here
Implementation tasks, meeting notes without decisions, or undocumented assumptions.

Use `template.md` when creating a new ADR.
